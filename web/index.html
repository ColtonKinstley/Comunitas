Why changing together matters

Most of what shortens healthy lives is changeable. The hard part isn't knowing what to do — it's doing it, week after week. That's the gap Comunitas is built for.

40%

Nearly two in five healthy years lost are linked to modifiable risks

Physical activity, smoking, diet and alcohol account for almost 40% of England's total disease burden.

Healthier routines are associated with extra years of life

Men +6.3 years
Women +7.6 years

Life expectancy at 45 in the healthiest lifestyle group versus the least healthy, across 480,940 UK adults — even among people living with multiple conditions. Not smoking had the largest individual association.
