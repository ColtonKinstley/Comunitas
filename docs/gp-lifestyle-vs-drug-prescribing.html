1 · The scale gap

Annual volume: drug items vs structured lifestyle referrals

Structured lifestyle referrals (social prescribing + Diabetes Prevention Programme + digital weight management) total well under 0.5% of prescription-item volume — about one lifestyle referral per 220 GP appointments, versus ~3.4 drug items per appointment. The lifestyle bar is drawn at minimum visible width; the true ratio is ~740:1.

Sources: NHSBSA Prescription Cost Analysis 2024/25 · Lancet Public Health 2025 · NHS England programme statistics. Lifestyle total is a derived estimate.

2 · Advice inside the consultation

Share of eligible patients who actually receive lifestyle advice or support

Advice tracks payment incentives: smoking advice (QOF-incentivised) reaches half of smokers, while alcohol advice reaches almost nobody. Verbal advice is under-recorded, so these are best-available recall/record figures — treat as approximate.

Sources: BJGP 2016 (n=15,252) · Addiction 2020 · BJGP 2017 systematic review · PMC 2016 alcohol screening studies.

3 · Guidance says lifestyle-first; volumes say otherwise

Depression pathway, England (people per year)

NICE recommends talking therapies and exercise for mild-to-moderate depression. In practice, ~13 people receive antidepressants for every one who completes a course of NHS talking therapy.

4 · The referral funnel

NHS Diabetes Prevention Programme: from referral to completion

The characteristic shape of every NHS lifestyle programme: most attrition happens before the programme even starts. Completing is associated with a 37% reduction in diabetes risk; even referral alone is associated with a 20% reduction.

Sources: Valabhji et al., Diabetes Care 2020 · Diabetic Medicine scoping review 2022 · Ravindrarajah et al., PLOS Medicine 2023.

5 · Adherence: neither side looks good

Sticking with it: medication adherence vs lifestyle programme completion

30–50% of medicines for long-term conditions aren't taken as recommended (NICE) — so ~50% adherence is the honest drug-side benchmark. Lifestyle completion among people who start is in the same range; the difference is the pre-start leak shown in chart 4, which drug statistics never capture.

Sources: NICE CG76/WHO 2003 · Diabetes Care 2020 · Obesity 2024 · Pavey HTA 2011 · NACR 2017/18 · Practical Diabetes 2018.

6 · Outcomes when lifestyle is properly supported

Smoking: validated abstinence one year after the quit attempt

NHS stop-smoking support roughly doubles long-term quit success — from a low base. (The headline "53.8% quit rate" reported by services is self-reported at just 4 weeks; the honest 12-month, carbon-monoxide-validated figure is shown here.)

Sources: ELONS study, NIHR 2016 · NCBI review of unaided quitting · NHS England stop smoking statistics 2023/24.

Preventing type 2 diabetes: lifestyle beats the drug head-to-head

In the landmark US Diabetes Prevention Program trial (n=3,234, cited in UK NICE guidance), intensive lifestyle intervention nearly doubled the risk reduction achieved by metformin. UK real-world echo: DiRECT achieved 46% diet-induced diabetes remission at 1 year in trial conditions; the NHS rollout achieves 32% among completers.

Source: Knowler et al., NEJM 2002 — reduction in diabetes incidence vs placebo over ~2.8 years.
